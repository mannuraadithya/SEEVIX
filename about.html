Who We Are

Technology for Better Cities

SEEVIX is an AI-powered civic issue reporting platform designed to bridge the gap between citizens and local authorities. It helps identify, report and track everyday civic problems through intelligent technology. Our goal is to make civic reporting simpler, faster and more accessible.

What SEEVIX Offers

Smart Issue Reporting

Submit civic complaints with descriptions, images and relevant details.

AI-Powered Detection

Uses computer vision to identify potholes and streetlight-related issues.

Automatic Classification

AI analyses complaint descriptions and suggests categories and priorities.

Interactive Issue Map

View reported civic problems through a location-based map.

Complaint Tracking

Monitor the progress and status of submitted complaints.

Citizen Participation

Encourage citizens to participate in improving their communities.

Our Mission

To empower citizens, improve civic accountability and contribute towards building cleaner, safer and smarter communities through technology and artificial intelligence.
